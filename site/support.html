Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. Suit Up! has no accounts and no server that stores your suits, sessions, or photos.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Data is stored by the app on your device. Your device backup (iCloud or Google backup) may include it, depending on your backup settings.

Why does the app ask for my location?

To detect when you are at a con, meet, or venue, so the app can tag your session for you. Location is used only while the app is open, and your coordinates are not sent to the developer. You can say no and pick events manually.

How do I turn off analytics?

Go to Settings -> About and turn off Share anonymous usage data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. This removes all suits, sessions, events, and photos from your device.
